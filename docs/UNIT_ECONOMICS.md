# Pilot Unit Economics

Collector net earnings per lot:

`sale value − transport − time/data costs − collector platform fees + pickup/reward benefit`

Pilot policy: collector fee is ₹0; cash remains supported. Replace the illustrative values below with observed field data before submission.

| Scenario (illustrative only) | Informal route | Formal platform route |
|---|---:|---:|
| Sale value | ₹1,000 | ₹1,120 |
| Transport | −₹80 | ₹0 (pickup) |
| Time/data cost | −₹60 | −₹30 |
| Collector platform fee | ₹0 | ₹0 |
| Pickup/reward benefit | ₹0 | +₹20 |
| Net collector earnings | ₹860 | ₹1,110 |

Illustrative uplift is ₹250 per lot. Sensitivity must be shown for price uplift, pickup cost and rejection/dispute rate.

Candidate platform revenue: recycler subscription, a recycler-side completed-transaction fee, or EPR-partner support. Track contribution margin as revenue minus messaging, hosting, storage, verification, support, failed pickup and payment costs. Do not charge collectors during the pilot.
