# Field Research Protocol

Required participants: at least 2 working scrap collectors/aggregators and 1 recycler. Do not enter real names, phone numbers, exact home addresses or identity documents in this repository.

Obtain recorded consent before observation. Record a participant code, broad operating area, preferred language, device class and role. Ask each collector to independently: photograph/categorize a lot, explain the estimate, select a recycler, confirm a handover and find a payment. Test Hindi or Marathi, pictorial safety guidance and loss/restoration of connectivity.

For every task record completion, time, assistance, errors, the participant’s explanation in their own words and an anonymized observation. The two collector acceptance gates are: all 5 tasks completed independently, and both explain that the displayed value is an estimate rather than a guaranteed final price.

Store consent evidence and recordings outside Git in an access-controlled research folder. Commit only an anonymized findings summary, prioritized changes and the date each change was retested.
